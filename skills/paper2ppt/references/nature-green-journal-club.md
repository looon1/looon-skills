# Nature 墨绿·组会精讲版

Use this reference only when `style_id` is `nature_green_journal_club`, the user asks for the approved green version, or a supplied reference deck clearly uses this system.

## Identity

- Chinese label: `Nature 墨绿·组会精讲版`.
- English handle: `Nature Forest Green Journal Club`.
- Purpose: formal biomedical group-meeting or journal-club presentation with full Results coverage.
- Palette: white canvas, forest green `#006B3C`, dark green `#004F2C`, pale result field `#EEF6F1`, neutral ink `#1F2E27`, light rule `#D6E3DC`.
- Use campus, hospital, or laboratory imagery only when the user supplied or authorized it. Do not bundle a user's reference image into the Skill.

## Slide grammar

1. Cover: one strong paper title, original English title or citation beneath, restrained institutional banner when available.
2. Contents: group the paper into a few major argument chapters rather than listing every Results subsection.
3. Background: one research question plus only the cohort and comparison facts needed to understand Result 1.
4. Results divider: a wide reference-derived banner, dominant `RESULT N`, and the exact Results subsection or faithful Chinese translation. The divider is navigation, not evidence.
5. Evidence slide: one objective conclusion title; one dominant panel or coherent panel pair; one pale-green strip containing one or two panel-matched observations; source footer and page number.
6. Continuation: adjacent slides may repeat the same conclusion title when multiple panels jointly establish one local conclusion. Do not invent a new micro-heading just to avoid repetition.
7. Closing: after every Results panel, show the end-to-end technical route. When the paper supplies a visual abstract or mechanism summary, place it after the route recap as the final slide. Nothing follows it unless the user explicitly requests Q&A or thanks.

## Type and density

- Evidence title: approximately 27–35 pt in the accepted reference system; keep it visibly larger than body copy and on one line when possible.
- Result divider number: visually dominant, normally at least 42 pt.
- Evidence area: normally 60–75% of usable canvas.
- Visible explanation: at most two short objective result statements. Put methods, reading order, cautious interpretation, and transitions in notes.
- A small panel centered inside a large empty frame is a failure. Split, reacquire, or use a disclosed locator-plus-zoom treatment.

## Prohibited drift

- No visible labels such as `解释边界`, `证据边界`, `本页结论`, or `讲解要点` unless present in the approved reference.
- No decorative side rail on evidence slides.
- No card-dashboard treatment, ornamental gradients, stickers, fake journal chrome, or social-media decoration.
- Do not place the study workflow or visual abstract near the beginning merely because it is an overview.
- Do not replace the paper's original visual abstract with a simplified reconstruction when the source version is readable.

## Notes

For each evidence slide: locate the visible panel, state the comparison or assay, give the reading order, report the objective observation, state only the necessary evidence boundary in natural academic language, and transition to the next analysis. The final visual-abstract note should replay the upper and lower branches and close without adding a new claim.
